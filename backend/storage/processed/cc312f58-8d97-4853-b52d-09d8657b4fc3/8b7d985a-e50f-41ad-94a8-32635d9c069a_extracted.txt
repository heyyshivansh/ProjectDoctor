LegalVault Project Proposal
1. Problem Statement
Legal teams and individuals often manage contracts, case documents, notices, and supporting files across
disconnected folders. Finding the correct document and understanding what it contains can be slow and error-prone.
2. Proposed Solution
LegalVault is a document management application for organizing legal documents in one searchable workspace.
Users can upload documents, assign metadata, search documents, and review document details.
3. Target Users
Legal professionals
Students or researchers working with legal documents
Individuals maintaining personal legal records
4. Core Features
Document upload
Document categorization
Metadata management
Search
Document preview
Basic access control
5. Scope
The initial system focuses on document organization, retrieval, and metadata. Advanced legal advice, automated
legal conclusions, and autonomous legal decision-making are outside the initial scope.
6. Expected Outcome
Users should be able to store legal documents in an organized workspace and retrieve relevant documents without
manually searching through unrelated files.
